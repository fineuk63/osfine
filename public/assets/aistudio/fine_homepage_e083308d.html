FINE Security Shield

Protection built around your control.

Privacy by design

Your device. Your data. Your choice.

FINE's intended privacy model is transparent: ask before accessing sensitive features, collect only what is needed and give people clear controls.

01 / PERMISSIONS

No hidden camera or microphone use

Camera, microphone, location and file access should require clear permission and visible indicators.

02 / DATA CONTROL

Clear privacy settings

Give users control over history, cookies, saved data, sync and optional diagnostics.

03 / SECURE DESIGN

Protect information

Use encryption in transit and at rest, secure authentication and minimal data collection.
